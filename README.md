# PermissionWise

### See what changed. Know what it means.

**Understand · Compare · Verify**

PermissionWise is an independent project that aims to make Android application permissions and privacy practices easier to understand.

Every day, we grant applications access to sensitive parts of our devices — contacts, location, camera, microphone and more. But do we really understand why these permissions are requested, what they mean for our privacy, or how they change between application versions?

PermissionWise aims to bridge this gap through evidence-backed explanations, privacy comparisons and accessible insights.

## The Problem

Understanding app permissions and privacy policies isn't always easy for everyday users.

- Why does an application need access to certain device resources?
- What does a particular permission actually allow?
- Have an application's permissions changed between versions?
- Has its privacy policy changed how it collects, shares or retains data?
- How can users distinguish verified information from assumptions?

PermissionWise aims to make these questions easier to answer.

## What PermissionWise Aims to Do

**Understand**

Explain Android application permissions in simple language, with context, supporting evidence and uncertainty indicators.

**Compare**

Compare application permission records and privacy-policy versions to identify additions, removals and meaningful changes.

**Verify**

Connect findings to reliable sources, distinguish facts from inferences and communicate uncertainty transparently.

## Key Features

- Application search and permission analysis.
- APK-based permission inspection.
- Contextual, evidence-linked permission explanations.
- Application-version permission comparison.
- Privacy-policy comparison and summarisation.
- Transparent risk indicators and actionable recommendations.
- English and Kannada explanations, with scope for additional languages.

*These represent the intended capabilities of the project and will be implemented progressively.*

## Technology

The technology stack is being explored and will be selected based on research and implementation requirements.

The project will investigate Android development, Python, permission-analysis tools, natural language processing, pretrained models and suitable AI components.
